How Wipro's Intra-Company Transfer (ICT) works — step by step
The ICT process — what actually happens
1
Wipro nominates her for a Canada project
Usually happens when a client project opens in Canada and they need people. Her manager recommends her, HR approves. Being visible, skilled, and clearly willing helps. Sankari contacting HR proactively is the right move.
2
Wipro applies for her work permit (LMIA-exempt)
Intra-company transfers don't need Labour Market Impact Assessment — much faster than regular work permits. Wipro's immigration lawyer handles everything. Processing: 2–8 weeks. She gets a Closed Work Permit tied to Wipro Canada.
3
Madhan applies for Spousal Open Work Permit simultaneously
Once Sankari's work permit is approved, Madhan applies for Spousal OWP — he can work for ANY Canadian employer. Apply together to save time. Processing: 8–16 weeks currently. His Node.js + Kafka + ENBD banking experience = CAD 100–130K in Toronto tech.
4
Arrive in Canada — first 3 months are an adjustment
Get: SIN number (Social Insurance Number), bank account, health card (provincial — takes 3 months to activate). Wipro usually provides relocation support (flights, 1–2 months temporary housing). Start frugal, build up gradually.
5
Madhan finds a job (Month 2–4 after arriving)
With Spousal OWP, Madhan applies to any Canadian employer. His stack (Node.js, Kafka, microservices, fintech + banking domain) is directly relevant to Toronto's tech sector — TD Bank, RBC, Shopify, Wealthsimple all hire for this. Realistic target: CAD 100–120K.
6
PR via Canadian Experience Class after 1 year
After 1 year of skilled work in Canada, both are eligible for CEC PR. With Sankari's M.Tech + Madhan's experience, CRS score likely 460–490+. Invitation to Apply (ITA) expected within 3–6 months of creating Express Entry profile. PR = permanent right to live and work in Canada.
Key questions to ask Wipro HR — don't leave any unanswered
Ask these in writing (email) — get answers before agreeing to anything
Q1
What is the salary band for QA engineers transferred to Canada?
Wipro typically pays slightly below Canadian market for ICT transfers. Know the exact number before agreeing. Research market rate: CAD 75–90K for Senior QA Automation Engineer in Toronto.
Q2
Is it a deputation or permanent transfer?
Deputation = temporary (2–3 years), remain on India payroll + Canada allowance. Permanent = move to Canada entity permanently. Different tax implications, different PF treatment, different PR pathway implications.
Q3
Does Wipro cover relocation costs?
Flights for 2 people, temporary accommodation (1–2 months), shipping allowance? Get this in writing — can save ₹2–3L in immediate costs.
Q4
What happens to 5+ years of India PF and gratuity?
After 5 years at Wipro, gratuity is due. Clarify: does going to Canada affect gratuity payout? For PF: as a non-resident she should withdraw or transfer — cannot continue contributing to EPF from abroad.
Q5
Can my spouse get a work permit?
Wipro should know this — confirm that as an ICT transferee, her spouse is eligible for a Spousal Open Work Permit. This is a legal right under Canadian immigration law, not a favour to be negotiated.
